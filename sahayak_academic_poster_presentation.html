1. Clinical Motivation & Problem Statement

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) affects nearly 1 billion individuals worldwide, causing recurrent nocturnal upper airway collapse, hypoxemia, sympathetic autonomic surges, and severe cardiovascular morbidity.

  • The Clinical Bottleneck: Gold-standard Polysomnography (PSG) costs $1,000–$2,500/night, requires 20+ intrusive wired sensors, and suffers from 6–12 month hospital waiting queues.
  • Localization Gap: Under-resourced regional populations lack native Bengali medical awareness systems and continuous low-cost home monitoring solutions.

4. Dual ECG & EDR Signal Processing

Single-lead electrocardiography captures dual physiological modalities without requiring additional respiratory belts:

  • Lead-II Electrocardiogram: Filtered via 0.5–45 Hz zero-phase Butterworth bandpass to eliminate baseline wander and high-frequency electromyographic noise.
  • ECG-Derived Respiration (EDR): Derived by tracking respiratory modulation of QRS R-peak amplitudes. During apnea, the thoracic expansion stops, causing immediate EDR Flatline Cessation.
🧠

5. SleepApneaNet: 1D-CNN + BiLSTM + Attention

Hierarchical neural architecture optimized for lightweight, real-time edge and cloud deployment:

1️⃣ Multi-Scale 1D-CNN: 3 Conv Blocks (Kernels: 15, 11, 7) + BatchNorm + GELU + MaxPool
2️⃣ Bidirectional LSTM: 2-Layer BiLSTM (Hidden: 64×2 = 128) capturing forward/backward autonomic dynamics
3️⃣ Self-Attention Layer: Computes temporal weights $\alpha_t = \text{Softmax}(w^T \tanh(W h_t))$
4️⃣ Dense Classifier Head: Dropout(0.4) + Linear(64, 2) outputting Apnea vs Normal probabilities

• Total Parameters: ~128,000 (Ultra-lightweight)
• Optimization: AdamW, Cosine Annealing, Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss
