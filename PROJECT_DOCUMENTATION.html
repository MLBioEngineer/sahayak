1. Executive Abstract

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) is an under-diagnosed respiratory sleep disorder affecting approximately 1 billion individuals globally, characterized by recurrent nocturnal pharyngeal airway collapse, intermittent hypoxemia, autonomic sympathetic surges, and severely increased risks of stroke, refractory hypertension, and sudden cardiac death. The gold-standard diagnostic modality, in-laboratory overnight Polysomnography (PSG), presents an unsustainable clinical bottleneck due to exorbitant costs ($1,000–$2,500/night), extreme physical intrusiveness (over 20 attached sensors), and 6–12 month hospital waiting queues.

Project সহায়ক (Sahayak) introduces an end-to-end, ultra-low-cost (~$15 Bill of Materials), wearable telemedicine platform that democratizes clinical sleep apnea screening using a single Lead-II electrocardiogram (ECG) channel. The system features a custom IoT biopotential acquisition node powered by a dual-core ESP32 and an Analog Devices AD8232 front-end, operating with a strict 100 Hz hardware timer interrupt. From the single ECG lead, the platform simultaneously extracts cardiac electrical activity and ECG-Derived Respiration (EDR) by reconstructing thoracic electrical impedance modulations, enabling direct visual confirmation of respiratory cessation flatlining during apnea events.

Diagnostic classification is powered by SleepApneaNet, an ultra-lightweight (~128,000 parameters) hierarchical deep learning architecture combining multi-scale 1D-CNN feature extraction, a 2-layer Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) network, and a multi-head temporal Self-Attention mechanism. Validated on the benchmark PhysioNet Apnea-ECG Database under strict, patient-isolated Subject-Wise Holdout Splitting (Zero Data Leakage), SleepApneaNet achieves 84.1% Accuracy, 86.4% Specificity, 81.7% Sensitivity, and 0.884 ROC-AUC, outperforming classical machine learning baselines while maintaining an inference latency of just 18 ms. The platform incorporates 1D-GradCAM saliency heatmaps to provide clinical interpretability, nonlinear Poincaré Heart Rate Variability (HRV) autonomic matrices, a 60 FPS HTML5 Canvas real-time ICU oscilloscope, an automated vector PDF clinical report generator, and a localized Bengali Medical AI Chatbot for patient triage.

2. Clinical Motivation & The Diagnostic Bottleneck

Over 936 million adults aged 30–69 years suffer from Obstructive Sleep Apnea worldwide (Lancet Respiratory Medicine). Despite severe cardiovascular risks, over 80% remain undiagnosed. Traditional Polysomnography (PSG) requires:

  • Excessive Financial Burden: $1,000 to $2,500 per overnight hospital sleep test.
  • Physical Intrusiveness: 22+ wired sensors (EEG, EOG, EMG, ECG, nasal airflow, chest/abdominal belts) disrupting natural sleep ("first-night effect").
  • Hospital Waitlists: 6 to 12 months in tertiary facilities.
  • Geographic Exclusion: Non-existent in semi-urban and rural clinics.
The Sahayak Solution: Slashing capital expenditure from $2,500 to a $15 reusable wearable node that extracts dual ECG + EDR physiological channels from only 3 adhesive chest electrodes.

4. Hardware Engineering & Circuit Design

The edge acquisition node utilizes an ESP32-WROOM-32 dual-core microcontroller (240 MHz) paired with an Analog Devices AD8232 biopotential analog front-end:

  • Core 1 (Deterministic Acquisition): Operates a strict 100 Hz hardware timer interrupt sampling GPIO 36 (ADC1_CH0) with zero software jitter.
  • Core 0 (Networking & Buffer): Asynchronously manages Wi-Fi TCP/IP connectivity and serializes 100-sample batches every second.
  • Einthoven Lead-II Montage: Right Arm (RA, sub-clavicular right), Left Arm (LA, sub-clavicular left), and Right Leg (RL, right abdomen) connected to the AD8232 Driven Right Leg (DRL) circuit to actively cancel 50/60 Hz powerline hum.

5. Digital Signal Processing: Dual ECG & EDR Respiration

  • Zero-Phase Butterworth Filter: 4th-order 0.5–45.0 Hz forward-backward digital bandpass filter eliminating baseline wander and high-frequency EMG noise without phase distortion.
  • Pan-Tompkins Algorithm: Five-point derivative, nonlinear squaring, 150 ms moving integration, and adaptive dual thresholding to detect R-peaks.
  • ECG-Derived Respiration (EDR): Lung inflation alters transthoracic electrical impedance, modulating R-peak heights. Cubic spline interpolation across R-peak amplitudes reconstructs the continuous thoracic breathing curve. During apnea, thoracic movement halts, causing EDR Flatline.
  • Poincaré HRV Analysis: Phase-space mapping of $RR_n$ vs $RR_{n+1}$. Normal breathing forms a compact "Comet" ellipse ($SD_1=22.8\text{ ms}, SD_2=52.4\text{ ms}$). Severe apnea disperses into a wide "Torpedo" pattern ($SD_1=58.6\text{ ms}, SD_2=114.2\text{ ms}$).

6. Deep Learning Architecture: SleepApneaNet

`SleepApneaNet` is a specialized lightweight neural network (~128,000 parameters) engineered for physiological time-series:

  1. Multi-Scale 1D-CNN: Three Conv blocks with wide kernels (15, 11, 7) + BatchNorm1d + GELU + MaxPool1d extracting morphological QRS wave anomalies.
  2. Bidirectional LSTM: 2-Layer BiLSTM (hidden 64×2 = 128) capturing bidirectional sequence dependencies across consecutive cardiac cycles.
  3. Temporal Self-Attention: $\alpha_t = \text{Softmax}(w^T \tanh(W h_t))$ dynamically concentrates neural weights on the onset of airway collapse.
  4. Dense Classifier: Linear(128, 64) $\rightarrow$ Dropout(0.4) $\rightarrow$ Linear(64, 2) outputting calibrated probabilities.
